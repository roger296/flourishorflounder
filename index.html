What I do

No jargon. No 40-page reports nobody reads.

Most finance advice for founders is either too basic to be useful, or so full of jargon it might as well be a foreign language.

I've spent 20 years as a CFO — through start-up, high growth, acquisitions, an AIM flotation and exits — mostly in e-commerce and retail businesses just like yours. I know what it's like to grow fast and still stare at the bank balance wondering where the money went.

Now I work with founder-led businesses to build genuine cash flow clarity, stronger financial foundations, and — when the time comes — a business that's actually ready to sell.

Just the numbers, explained properly, and a plan you can act on.

How we work together

Three ways in, depending on what you need.

Fractional CFO

Ongoing, in the business. I own the numbers alongside you — cash flow, reporting, the finance team — without you carrying a full-time CFO salary.

Board Advisor or NED

A seat at the table. Governance, challenge and a second experienced head in the room for the decisions that actually move the needle.

A defined project

One thing, done properly. A funding round, a sale, due diligence, or rebuilding the reporting so you can finally see what's going on.

Typically one to two days a month, flexed around what's happening in the business. Tell me what's going on and I'll tell you honestly whether you need me, and what that would look like.

Where I'm most useful

Someone who's been through it before, beside you.

Cash flow and exit-readiness are where most people find me — but 20 years across start-up, high growth, acquisitions, an AIM flotation and multiple exits means the list is longer. I've raised debt and equity, negotiated deals on both sides of the table, built stock forecasting and logistics systems from scratch, cut costs, restructured teams, and sat on boards through everything from rebrands to shareholder disputes.

If your problem is "we're growing but something feels off and I can't quite name it," that's usually exactly the kind of mess I'm most useful in.

Multi-currency, multi-channel, multi-warehouse complexity is where I've spent most of my career. If your finances have outgrown a single neat spreadsheet, that's familiar territory, not a special case.

Why work with me

I'm not guessing what a buyer cares about. I've been one.

Most advisers have done the operator job or the advisory job — rarely both, and rarely across the full lifecycle of a business.

I've been the CFO in the room during 200%+ growth, the one steering a flotation, the one restructuring a business that was struggling, and the one on the other side of the table doing due diligence on an acquisition. So I'm not guessing what a buyer, investor or board will actually care about — I've been all three.

If you want someone who'll hand you a report, there are plenty of options. If you want someone who'll make sure you understand what's happening in your business — and can hold your own in that boardroom conversation without me in the room — that's the bit I do differently.

What it's like to work with me

Explained the way I'd explain it to a mate over coffee.

I'll walk you through your own numbers with a whiteboard, a napkin, whatever's to hand — until it actually clicks, not just until you've nodded politely enough times that I move on.

I ask a lot of "why" questions, because half the time the real problem isn't the number itself — it's the decision hiding behind it.

Expect direct, honest input rather than avoiding the awkward stuff. I'd rather have that conversation early and informally than let it become a crisis you find out about from your bank balance.
